What you do

Build up. Fill it. Keep it running.

  • Build it up

    Draw floors, place rooms in rows and stretch elevator shafts. Go up to 100 floors above the lobby, and dig down for venues underground.

  • Little lives

    Tenants move in, go to work, eat out and come home. Every room has its own routine, from the boba counter to the karate class.

  • Pleasant chaos

    Elevator queues grow and hotels need housekeeping. When a fire starts, the fire crew arrives with ladders and hoses.
