Preview text
Three properties. Three disasters. One last chance in Hanami Bay.
Kanemochi Holdings · Office of the CEO
●  Incoming call from Mr. Kanemochi  ·  CEO, Kanemochi Holdings

I have read your file.

A mall with no doors. A parking garage with no ramp. A hotel whose rooftop pool now lives in the lobby.

A monkey in a tiny suit rises into the corner of the frame.

That is Senior Vice President Saru. He outranks you. He has also read your file.

Kanemochi Holdings owns an empty lot in Hanami Bay. It is a city we built to look like Tokyo. It does not work like Tokyo. Neither do you.

You will build a tower there. Floors. Rooms. Elevators. People who pay rent and stay.

This is your last assignment. Do not embarrass me again.

When you fail, and you will fail, I want it in writing.

Saru gives a thumbs down.

My assistant will tell you when the lot opens to the public. Until then, the site foreman will be in touch. She is cheerful. I have asked her to stop.

Preview text

Hanami Bay site · Foreman's radio
●  Incoming call from Marisol  ·  Site foreman, Hanami Bay

Boss! Marisol here, your site foreman. I am writing from a beam, so I will keep it short.

Welcome to Hanami Bay. It looks like Tokyo until you try to use it.

Three rules before you start.

One: build up. Every tower starts as a lobby and $180,000. Draw a floor, then draw the next one. You can go 100 floors up and dig down for venues underground.

Two: fill it. Homes, offices, a café, a ramen bar, an arcade. Every room has its own little people with their own routines.

Three: an elevator or nothing. A room only earns if an elevator reaches its floor. No elevator, no customers, no rent. I worked on your mall. I asked about the doors twice.

One strange thing. This lot sat empty for eleven years. Even the pigeons go around it. Nobody will tell me why.

Something metal falls in the distance.

Oh no. The beam. Gotta go!

Every room in your tower runs on its own clock. People go to work, eat out and come home. Things go wrong in small, silly ways, and you fix them.

I have reviewed this approach. I call it adequate. Saru calls it cozy. I am reviewing Saru.
