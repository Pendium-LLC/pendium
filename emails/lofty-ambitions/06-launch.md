The board has asked me to announce something. I will read it as written.

"The Hanami Bay lot is open. Lofty Ambitions is available today."

*He sets the paper down.*

You now have everything you need. An empty lot. $180,000. A city that looks like Tokyo. Elevators, if you can find them.

The ramen bar is waiting for its lights. The meatballs are waiting to escape. Your sister is waiting to tell everyone at dinner.

I have one instruction. Open one room that pays rent. Even you can find an elevator.

*Saru peeks up from the corner of the frame. He does not give a thumbs down. He is thinking about it.*

Do not embarrass me again.
