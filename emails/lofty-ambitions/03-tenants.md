Your tenants have written to me. I will read the complaints aloud.

"The ramen bar opens late and plays a song. The song is good. I stay too long." This is not a complaint. It is a confession.

"Ducks moved into the garden." Call the park ranger.

"The giant meatballs rolled away from the pizza parlor." Call the cleanup crew. Do not ask me why the meatballs are giant.

"The shops close at night and the windows go gold. I watched the sun set from the 40th floor." Nobody asked you to make it pleasant.

"My neighbor rides the elevator to every floor to say hello." Elevators are not for saying hello. Build more of them.

*Saru eats a banana in the corner of the frame.*

Every room in your tower runs on its own clock. People go to work, eat out and come home. Things go wrong in small, silly ways, and you fix them.

I have reviewed this approach. I call it adequate. Saru calls it cozy. I am reviewing Saru.
