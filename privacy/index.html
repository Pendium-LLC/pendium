Who and what this covers

Pendium, LLC is a Missouri limited liability company. In this policy, "Pendium", "we", and "us" mean Pendium, LLC.

This policy covers:

  • this website, pendium.co;
  • email you exchange with us;
  • phone calls and text messages between you and Pendium; and
  • the Lofty Ambitions mailing list.

Our products have their own privacy policies. When you use a product, that product's policy applies to it:

  • Hollerback privacy policy
  • Emeri privacy policy

What we collect

When you visit this website

We run no analytics on pendium.co, and we set no cookies. The companies that deliver the site receive your IP address and browser details, as every web host does. The section Who we share it with names them.

When you email us

We keep your email address, your name if you give it, and what you write.

When you become a customer, ask for support, or work with us

  • Your name, your business name, your email address, and your mobile phone number.
  • The details of your request, your project, your job, or your account.
  • A record of how and when you agreed to be contacted by phone and text.

When we call or text you

  • The texts we send you and the texts you send us, with the time of each one.
  • A recording and a written transcript of each call, and a short written result.
  • Call times, call lengths, and whether each call was answered.
  • Your request to stop calls or texts.

When you join the Lofty Ambitions mailing list

We keep your email address and the date you signed up.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, bank details, or health information by email, call, or text. Please do not send them to us that way. We do not buy personal information from data brokers.

How we use it

  • To answer you and to do the work you asked for.
  • To contact you about your request, your job, or your account.
  • To send the Lofty Ambitions emails you signed up for.
  • To honor requests to stop calls, texts, or emails.
  • To keep records that tax and business law require.
  • To find and stop fraud, abuse, and security problems.
  • To meet legal duties and to answer lawful requests from authorities.

We use personal information only for these purposes. We do not sell it. We do not use it for advertising. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not use your calls, texts, or emails with us to train AI models.

Text messages

You get texts from Pendium only if you gave us your mobile number and agreed to be contacted by phone and text. People give us their number when they become a customer, open a support request, or are hired. We text you only about that request, your job, or your account. We do not send marketing texts.

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or your text message consent with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Every category of sharing in this policy excludes text message opt-in data and consent. We share that information with no one, except the providers that deliver our texts, and only so they can deliver them.
  • Message frequency varies with your request. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts and calls. Reply START to allow them again. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@pendium.co.
  • An AI assistant writes some of our texts. Each conversation says so in its first text.

Our terms of service have the full text message terms.

Phone calls

An AI voice assistant places some of our calls. It says that it is an AI assistant at the start of each conversation. It also says that the call is recorded and may be monitored. If you do not want to be recorded, end the call and email us instead. To stop calls, tell the assistant to stop calling you, or email us.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the providers that help us run Pendium. Each one gets only the data it needs for its job, and may use it only to provide that service to us.

  • GitHub. Hosts this website.
  • Cloudflare. Runs our domain name records and routes some visits to the site.
  • Google. Provides our email. It also serves the fonts on this site, so it receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Buttondown. Stores the Lofty Ambitions mailing list and sends its emails.
  • Hollerback. Places our calls and sends our texts. Hollerback is our own product. Its privacy policy lists the providers that carry calls and texts, such as phone carriers and speech services.

We also share information in three other cases:

  • when the law requires it, such as a valid court order;
  • when it is needed to protect a person from serious harm; and
  • if we sell or merge the business. The buyer must follow this policy for the information it receives, and we will tell you before your information moves.

When an authority asks for your information, we check that the request is lawful, and we give only what the request requires. We tell you about the request unless the law forbids it.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no advertising pixels, and no trackers that follow you across sites. Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing to opt out of. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we already treat you as opted out.

How long we keep it

  • We keep personal information only as long as its purpose needs it. Then we delete it or remove what identifies you.
  • We keep mailing list addresses until you unsubscribe.
  • We keep requests to stop calls, texts, or emails for as long as we operate, so that we keep honoring them.
  • We keep billing and tax records for as long as the law requires.
  • We keep records of consent to calls and texts for as long as the law lets a claim about them be brought.

Security

We limit access to personal information to the people who need it for their work. We use providers that encrypt data in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

You have these rights wherever you live:

  • See it. Ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Correct it. Ask us to fix information that is wrong.
  • Delete it. Ask us to delete your information.
  • Stop contact. Reply STOP to a text, tell the assistant on a call, or use the unsubscribe link in an email.
  • Withdraw consent. Take back any consent you gave us, at any time.

To use a right, email hello@pendium.co. We may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you. We answer within 45 days. If we cannot do what you ask, we tell you why, and you can ask us to review that decision. We keep some information when the law requires it, such as tax records and requests to stop contact.

We will not treat you differently because you used a right. You can have someone you authorize make a request for you.

Children

Pendium's website, mailing list, calls, and texts are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child gave us information, email us and we will delete it.

Where we process data

Pendium operates in the United States. We and our providers process personal information in the United States. If you contact us from another country, your information comes to the United States.

Changes to this policy

We post any change on this page and update the effective date. We will not reduce your rights under this policy for information we already hold without telling you first and, where the law requires it, asking for your consent.

Contact

Email hello@pendium.co with privacy questions or requests. Our terms of service cover the rest.
